Emotions & love

Every conflict is two people trying to meet their universal needs through ineffective strategies. Start at the roots, with what’s alive inside you. Then grow up through the horizon, out to the people, animals and world around you.

A grateful fun fact

The idea that feelings point to universal needs, and that conflicts live between strategies, grows out of Marshall B. Rosenberg’s Nonviolent Communication. The feelings and needs words are adapted from the Center for Nonviolent Communication’s inventories (cnvc.org). Thank you!

Mock-up notes

  • The tree is drawn in four slices, one per unit row, so each unit always lines up with its part of the tree, whatever the card height. On phones the tree shrinks to a narrow strip on the left.
  • The same model works for every course: roots (inner signals) → horizon → tree (acting in the world) → special situations.
